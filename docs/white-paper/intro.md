# Introduction

Qwest is a project management tool designed to address important but often overlooked challenges in task and project management.

This document explores common issues found in current tools and practices, offering solutions backed by research. The findings come from practical field studies and reviews of academic publications.

## Problem definition

### Limited availability for national businesses
Many task and project management tools are not accessible in Iran. These applications often block users entirely or provide only partial service due to various restrictions.

Additionally, managing large teams presents financial challenges for project managers. As team sizes grow, many tools require paid subscriptions. International monetary transactions are difficult to complete in Iran and are sometimes entirely impossible. The absence of pricing models tailored to purchasing power parity (PPP) further exacerbates these issues.

### Data ownership concerns
Although a variety of business models exist for project management tools, most popular options rely on cloud-based services where data is stored externally. While this approach is acceptable for some organizations, others prefer to maintain control of sensitive information by hosting data on their own servers.

### The need for offline support
Despite the widespread assumption of constant internet access, many areas lack reliable high-speed connections or experience temporary outages. Furthermore, some users simply prefer software that can function without an internet connection.

### Neglected user interface design
A well-designed and visually appealing user interface (UI) is essential for an optimal project management experience. However, UI design is often deprioritized in software development, resulting in less engaging and harder-to-use tools.
